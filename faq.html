Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Choose Farmer Sign Up or Client Sign Up from the home page and complete the registration form.

Can I buy directly from farmers?

Yes. FarmXT connects buyers directly with farmers without unnecessary middlemen.

How can I track my order?

Open the Track Order page from your dashboard to check your order status.

How can a farmer list products?

Farmers can log in, open the Produce Type page, enter product details, and submit the listing.

How do I contact FarmXT support?

Use the Contact page to send your questions or feedback to the FarmXT team.
