About FarmXT

FarmXT is a smart agriculture platform that directly connects farmers with buyers, helping them sell fresh produce without unnecessary middlemen. Our platform promotes transparency, fair pricing, and better opportunities for farmers while providing high-quality products to customers.

🌱 Our Mission

To empower farmers through technology by providing a secure, transparent, and user-friendly platform where they can connect directly with consumers and businesses.

🎯 Our Vision

To become India's most trusted digital agricultural marketplace that supports sustainable farming and improves farmers' income through innovation.

⭐ Why Choose FarmXT?

  • Direct Farmer to Customer Connection
  • Transparent Pricing
  • Secure Login System
  • Community Network for Farmers
  • Easy Product Listing
  • Order Tracking
  • Simple and Modern Interface
  • Future AI-based Crop Recommendations
